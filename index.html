I am an Assistant Professor (Tenure Track) in Building Energy Systems at LUT University, Finland.

My research focuses on building energy systems, HVAC, data centre cooling and thermal management, waste heat recovery, thermal energy storage, energy flexibility, and intelligent energy systems.

My work combines energy system modelling, building simulation, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), data analytics, optimisation, and artificial intelligence to develop energy-efficient and low-carbon buildings and energy infrastructure.

Research Interests

  • Building energy systems and HVAC
  • Data centre cooling and thermal management
  • Waste heat recovery and district heating
  • Thermal energy storage
  • Energy flexibility and demand response
  • AI and data-driven energy system optimisation

Current Research

My current research particularly focuses on the interaction between AI infrastructure, data centres, buildings, and energy systems, including advanced cooling technologies, waste heat utilisation, and integration with district heating systems.

Research Group

I lead the Building Energy Systems Research Group at LUT University. Our research covers building energy systems, intelligent HVAC, data centre energy systems, thermal management, and sustainable energy infrastructure.
