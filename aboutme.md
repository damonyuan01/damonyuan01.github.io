I am **Xiaolei (Damon) Yuan**, an Assistant Professor (Tenure Track) at **LUT University, Finland**. My research lies at the intersection of **building energy systems, sustainable AI infrastructure, thermal management, and artificial intelligence**.

My research aims to develop **energy-efficient, low-carbon, and intelligent built environments and infrastructure systems** by integrating physics-based modelling, data-driven methods, optimization, and experimental research.

## Research Interests

My current research focuses on four interconnected areas:

- **AI Infrastructure Thermal Management**  
  Data centre cooling, liquid cooling, airflow management, high-density computing environments, thermal management, and waste heat recovery.

- **Building Energy Systems**  
  HVAC systems, building energy efficiency, energy flexibility, thermal energy storage, demand response, and low-carbon energy systems.

- **AI and Data-Driven Energy Modelling**  
  Building energy prediction, machine learning, reinforcement learning, optimization, data analytics, and intelligent control.

- **District Energy and Waste Heat Integration**  
  Data centre waste heat, low-temperature district heating, sector coupling, renewable energy integration, and urban energy systems.

A major focus of my current work is **sustainable AI infrastructure**, particularly the interaction between rapidly growing AI/data centre infrastructure and building and urban energy systems. This includes advanced cooling technologies, waste heat utilization, energy flexibility, and the integration of data centres with district heating networks.

## Academic Profile

I received my **Doctor of Science (Technology)** in Energy Technology from **Aalto University, Finland**, in 2022. My doctoral research focused on the **optimization of row- and rack-level airflow management in data centres**.

Before joining LUT University, I worked as a **Postdoctoral Researcher at Aalto University** from 2022 to 2025 and subsequently continued my research as an independent researcher collaborating with Aalto University.

My academic background also includes:

- **D.Sc. (Tech.), Energy Technology**, Aalto University, Finland, 2022
- **D.Sc. Energy annd Environmental Protection**, Tongji University, Finland, 2022 (Certificate of Sudy)
- **M.Eng., Architecture and Civil Engineering**, Nanjing Tech University, China, 2019
- **M.Sc., Sustainable Built Environments**, University of Reading, UK, 2016

## Research Highlights

My research has resulted in **90 scientific publications**, including **71 peer-reviewed journal articles** and **19 conference papers**. My work has received more than **2,400 citations**, with an **h-index of 24**.

My publications cover topics including data centre cooling and waste heat recovery, building energy prediction, HVAC systems, thermal energy storage, district heating, demand response, and energy system optimization.

Selected research contributions include:

- Data centre waste heat recovery and its integration with district heating networks
- Airflow and thermal management in high-density data centres
- Advanced liquid cooling and thermal management technologies for AI infrastructure
- AI-based building energy prediction and intelligent energy management
- Thermal energy storage for flexible and low-carbon cooling systems
- Building and district energy system modelling and optimization
- Waste heat utilization from emerging energy and digital infrastructure
