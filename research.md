My research focuses on **building energy systems, sustainable AI infrastructure, and intelligent energy management**, combining physics-based modelling, data-driven methods, optimization, and experimental research.

## Research Areas

### Sustainable AI Infrastructure
Data centre and AI infrastructure thermal management, advanced air and liquid cooling, waste heat recovery, and integration with urban energy systems.

### Building Energy Systems
HVAC systems, building energy efficiency, energy flexibility, thermal energy storage, heat pumps, district heating, and renewable energy integration.

### AI for Energy Systems
Building energy prediction, machine learning, reinforcement learning, intelligent control, optimization, and hybrid physics-based/data-driven modelling.

### Waste Heat & District Energy
Recovery and utilization of waste heat from data centres, buildings, hydrogen production, and other infrastructure, with particular emphasis on district heating integration.

## Research Methods

CFD • Building Performance Simulation • Energy System Modelling • Machine Learning • Optimization • Reinforcement Learning • Experimental Measurements • Techno-economic Analysis
